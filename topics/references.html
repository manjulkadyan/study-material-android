Books

The following books are cited or recommended throughout the study guide.

"The Threading Book" (cited throughout topics.md)

The study guide repeatedly references a concurrency/Android threading book covering:

  • Part I — Process and thread fundamentals in Android
  • Producer-consumer patterns
  • Handler / Looper / MessageQueue internals
  • Thread-induced memory leaks (static inner classes, weak references)
  • Stopping and retaining worker threads
  • Cleaning up message queues

The exact title is not recorded in the source. The description most closely matches one of:

  • "Android Concurrency" — G. Blake Meike (covers HandlerThread, Looper, AsyncTask)
  • "Efficient Android Threading" — Anders Göransson (O'Reilly, ISBN 978-1449364137) — covers all cited topics and is the most widely referenced Android-specific threading book; most likely match

Search ISBN 9781449364137 or title "Efficient Android Threading" to locate it.

Kotlin

  • Effective Kotlin — Marcin Moskala (Kt. Academy)
    • Best practices for null safety, coroutines, collections, performance
    • https://kt.academy/book/effectivekotlin
  • Kotlin in Action — Dmitry Jemerov & Svetlana Isakova (Manning)
